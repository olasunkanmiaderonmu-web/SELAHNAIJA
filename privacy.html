Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

This policy explains what personal information Selah Naija (“we”, “us”) collects when you use this website, why we collect it, and the choices you have. We aim to follow applicable Nigerian data protection law, including the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023.

1. Information we collect

If you create an account

  • Your name and email address.
  • Your password. It is stored in scrambled (hashed) form by our login provider. We cannot see or read it.
  • A profile picture, if you choose to add one.
  • Your favourite songs, playlists, recently played list and download history.

If you only visit the website

  • You can listen to and download music and sermons without an account. We count plays and downloads of each song or sermon. These counts are not linked to you.

If you contact us, report content or join the newsletter

  • Your email address when you subscribe to the newsletter.
  • The details you send us in a contact message or a report.

Technical information

  • Your network (IP) address and browser details are processed briefly to keep the website secure and to stop abuse, such as automated spam. We do not use them for advertising.
  • Your browser stores small pieces of information that the website needs to work: your login session, your volume setting, the state of the music player, and announcements you have dismissed. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

2. How we use your information

  • To run the website, play and deliver music and sermons, and provide your account features.
  • To save your favourites, playlists and history.
  • To reply to your messages and deal with reports.
  • To send newsletter updates, if you subscribed. You can ask to be removed at any time.
  • To keep the website safe, find faults, and prevent misuse.

3. Why we are allowed to use it

We process your information because you gave consent (for example, when you create an account or subscribe), because it is needed to provide the service you asked for, and because we have a legitimate interest in keeping the website secure. You can withdraw your consent at any time.

4. Who can see your information

  • Our administrators can see account details (name, email) and account activity when needed for support, moderation and security.
  • Service providers that run the website for us: a cloud database, login and file storage provider (Supabase), our web hosting provider, and a content delivery network that serves website code libraries. They may only use your information to provide their service to us.
  • Authorities, if the law requires us to share information.

We do not sell your personal information.

5. Where information is stored

Our service providers may store information on servers outside Nigeria. When this happens, we choose providers that protect information to a high standard.

6. How long we keep it

We keep account information while your account is open. Contact messages and reports are kept for as long as needed to deal with them and for our records. Newsletter addresses are kept until you unsubscribe. When you ask us to delete your account, we delete your account information, favourites, playlists and history.

7. Your rights

You may ask us to: show you the information we hold about you; correct it; delete it; limit how we use it; give you a copy; or stop using it for newsletters. To make a request, use our Contact page. You also have the right to complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

8. Keeping information safe

Access to the database is protected by security rules, so people can only see their own account information. Audio files are protected by short-lived access links. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information and fix problems quickly.

9. Children

This website is not aimed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you are under 18, please use the website with a parent or guardian's permission. If you believe a child has given us personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed. If we make important changes, we will tell you on the website.

11. Contact us

Questions about this policy? Please visit our Contact page.
