If you believe your rights have been infringed

If you own or control the rights to something on this website and did not give permission, please tell us. Send your notice using the Report a problem page (choose “This is my work or I own the rights”) or through our Contact page. Please include:

  1. your name and how we can contact you (email and, if possible, phone);
  2. a description of the work you own, and proof or details of your ownership if you have them;
  3. the web address (link) of the page on our website where it appears;
  4. a statement that you believe in good faith that the use on our website is not authorised by you or by the law;
  5. a statement that the information in your notice is accurate, and that you are the rights owner or are allowed to act for the rights owner.

What we do next

  • We review notices promptly. Where a notice appears valid, we remove or disable access to the content while we look into it.
  • We may tell the person who provided the content that a notice was received.
  • If the content was removed by mistake, the person responsible may respond with an explanation, and we will review it fairly.
  • We may close the accounts of anyone who repeatedly infringes the rights of others.
