About Selah Naija

A home for Nigerian gospel music and inspiring messages.

Our story

“Selah” is a word found in the Psalms. Many believe it invites us to pause, reflect and lift our hearts. That is the spirit behind Selah Naija: a calm, beautiful place where Nigerians and gospel lovers around the world can stop for a moment and be refreshed by worship, praise and the Word.

What you will find here

  • Gospel music from Nigerian artists: worship, praise, gospel R&B, gospel rap and instrumentals.
  • Sermons from pastors, ministers and Christian speakers on faith, prayer, the Holy Spirit and daily Christian living.
  • Playlists for every moment, from morning worship to Sunday celebration.
  • Free streaming and downloads. You never need an account just to listen or download. An account lets you save favourites, build playlists and see your history.

Our commitment to artists and ministries

We only share music, sermons, pictures and lyrics that we have permission to distribute. Every song and sermon shows who made it. If you believe something on this website should not be here, please tell us using the Report a problem page. We take every report seriously. You can read how we handle copyright concerns in our Copyright Notice.
